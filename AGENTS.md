# Coleggo contributor instructions

- Use British English in user-facing copy.
- Do not use em dashes in user-facing copy.
- Run formatting, analysis and tests after meaningful changes.
- Do not add production dependencies without a clear reason.
- Do not commit secrets.
- Do not silently replace working functionality.
- Prefer a complete vertical slice over numerous unfinished features.
- Keep mock and production implementations clearly separated.
- Do not commit or push unless explicitly instructed.
