# Coleggo Privacy Notice (Draft)

**Status:** pre-launch draft for legal review. Replace all bracketed fields and obtain advice appropriate to the countries in which Coleggo is offered before publication.

**Last updated:** 20 July 2026

## Who is responsible for your information

[Coleggo legal entity name] is the controller of personal information processed through Coleggo. Contact us at [privacy contact email] or [postal address]. If we appoint a data protection officer, their contact details will be published here.

## Information we process

Coleggo processes information you provide when creating and using an account:

- account information, including workplace email address and password hash;
- profile information, including first name, initials, workplace, worksite, broad home area, commute corridor, commute days, journey timing, role, available seats and optional vehicle summary;
- commute-circle membership, requests, attendance, cancellations and in-app notifications;
- workplace-verification result; and
- technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as authentication tokens and service logs.

The current product design does not collect exact home addresses, telephone numbers, precise live location or precise pickup points. Before mutual acceptance, users see broad commute information rather than an employee directory.

## Why we use information

We use information to:

- create and secure accounts;
- match compatible commuting patterns and manage circles;
- verify access to an approved workplace email method;
- deliver requested product functions and notifications;
- investigate misuse, protect users and improve service reliability; and
- meet legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

Our lawful basis will depend on the processing activity and jurisdiction. Before launch, [Coleggo legal entity name] must document the applicable basis, which may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations.

## Who can see information

Other users receive only the information necessary for the commute feature. Coleggo is designed to show a broad area and route compatibility, rather than an exact address or precise location, before mutual acceptance.

We may share information with service providers that host or secure the product, subject to suitable contractual safeguards. We may also disclose information where legally required or to protect people, rights and property. We do not sell personal information.

## International transfers

If information is transferred outside the country where you live, we will use an appropriate transfer mechanism required by applicable law. Launch documentation must identify hosting locations and safeguards.

## Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described here, subject to legal, security and backup requirements. See `docs/data_retention_policy_draft.md` for the proposed operational schedule. Account deletion removes active account data from the application database, while protected backups expire on the documented schedule.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to a supervisory authority. You can request account deletion in the app when using the server-backed service, or contact [privacy contact email]. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information. No system is completely secure. Keep your account credentials confidential and report suspected unauthorised access to [security contact email].

## Children

Coleggo is not intended for children. The launch team must set an appropriate minimum age for each market and implement age-related controls where required.

## Changes

We may update this notice as the product or legal requirements evolve. We will publish the updated version and, where required, provide additional notice or request consent.
