# Coleggo Product Differentiation

Coleggo is not a taxi application, public ride marketplace or Uber competitor. It is a private workplace commuting network for verified colleagues who want dependable, recurring journeys around shared working patterns.

## Why Verified Workplaces

Discovery is bounded by organisation, approved worksite and compatible commuting context. Users discover commutes, not an employee directory. Workplace verification confirms access to an approved workplace verification method. It does not confirm driving ability, insurance, criminal history or personal character.

## Why Commute Circles

One-off lift requests are useful, but workplace commuting is mostly repetitive. Commute Circles make the recurring arrangement explicit: driver, optional backup driver, passengers, days, departure window, public meeting area, seat availability, confirmations and activity.

## Why Explainable Matching

Scores are deterministic and shown with reasons and limitations. Coleggo does not describe a match as suitable when essential requirements conflict, such as incompatible roles, different worksites or shift times that do not overlap.

## Why Exact Addresses Are Hidden

Discovery uses approximate areas, postcode districts and broad corridors. Exact home addresses and precise locations are not exposed before mutual acceptance. Meeting guidance favours public places such as station entrances, supermarket car parks, workplace shuttle stops and recognised landmarks.

## Why Behavioural Trust

Coleggo avoids superficial star ratings as the main trust mechanism. Reliability is represented through commuting behaviour such as completed commutes, recurring-circle participation, response consistency, late cancellations and no-shows. Warnings are private-by-default and only shown where relevant.

## Why Shift Workers Matter

The product supports early, late, night, standard and custom shifts, including overnight ranges such as 20:00 to 08:00. Matching considers days and shift windows so a colleague finishing at 20:00 is not overstated as a strong commute option for someone finishing at 17:00.

## Why No Fare Marketplace

The everyday product story is green workplace carpooling: colleagues on the same corridor share a recurring journey so fewer single-occupancy cars make the same trip.

The initial product focuses on workplace trust, recurring patterns, privacy and dependable planning. Dynamic fares, driver earnings, surge pricing and marketplace take-rates are intentionally postponed because they would change the regulatory, safety and product expectations.

Optional peer cost-sharing (for example contributing to fuel or parking) may be added later as a Circle agreement. That is different from turning Coleggo into a taxi marketplace. Until Circles, confirmations and trust boundaries work, money stays out of the critical path.
