# Privacy Product Principles

Coleggo follows one discovery rule:

> Users discover compatible commutes, not employee directories.

## Boundaries

- Users normally see colleagues from the same verified organisation and approved worksite.
- Shared business parks or partner organisations should require explicit approval.
- Discovery is route, schedule and role based.
- Arbitrary employee-name search is intentionally excluded.
- Exact home addresses, private phone numbers and private email addresses are not shown in discovery.

## Location Privacy

Before mutual acceptance, Coleggo shows:

- general area;
- postcode district;
- broad journey corridor;
- approximate proximity language;
- public meeting-point preference.

It does not show:

- exact home address;
- exact pickup point;
- precise live location;
- measured distance unless reliable route data exists.

## Meeting Points

Coleggo should encourage public meeting points such as train station entrances, supermarket car parks, public car parks, workplace shuttle stops and recognised landmarks. Home pickup must not be the default.

## Sensitive Requirements

Accessibility and practical journey requirements should be private by default. The product should reveal only the compatibility requirement needed for the commute, for example "Step-free pickup point required".

## Trust Statement

Workplace verification confirms access to an approved workplace verification method. It does not confirm driving ability, insurance, criminal history or personal character.
