# Coleggo Prototype Validation Plan

**Status:** ready to run. No user sessions have been recorded in this document yet.

## Goal

Validate that workplace commuters can understand Coleggo's privacy boundaries, complete the core commute-circle journey, and recognise the safety limitations before a wider pilot.

## Participant profile

Recruit 5 to 8 participants who:

- commute to the same workplace or shared worksite at least twice a week;
- represent both prospective drivers and passengers;
- use a mix of Android and iOS devices where possible; and
- have not taken part in the product build.

Do not ask participants to enter their real home address, personal telephone number, vehicle registration, insurance details or other sensitive information during prototype sessions.

## Session format

- **Duration:** 30 to 40 minutes.
- **Setting:** moderated remote or in-person session.
- **Environment:** prototype data only, or an isolated staging account.
- **Recorder:** obtain explicit consent before recording audio, video or screen activity.

## Facilitator script

1. Explain that this is a prototype test, not an assessment of the participant.
2. State that Coleggo is not a taxi, identity, safeguarding or insurance-verification service.
3. Ask the participant to think aloud. Do not coach them through the screens.
4. Remind them not to enter real sensitive information.
5. Collect only the minimum research notes needed for the study.

## Tasks

1. **Understand privacy**: Find the safety and privacy information. Explain what another commuter can see before mutual acceptance.
2. **Set up a commute**: Complete onboarding using fictional details and select a worksite, broad area, schedule and role.
3. **Evaluate a match**: Review a compatible commuter or circle and explain whether the information is enough to make a safe first contact decision.
4. **Request to join**: Send a circle request and identify what happens next.
5. **Manage a journey**: Confirm attendance, cancel a journey, or select a backup driver using the prototype flow.
6. **Control data**: Find reset or account deletion in Settings and explain the result of each action.
7. **Interpret safety limits**: Explain what workplace verification does and does not mean.

## Measures

Record for every task:

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Completion | Completed independently, completed with help, or not completed |
| Time | Time from task prompt to completion or abandonment |
| Errors | Incorrect route, incorrect interpretation or blocked action |
| Confidence | Participant rating from 1 (not confident) to 5 (very confident) |
| Safety comprehension | Participant can accurately state that Coleggo does not verify licences, insurance, identity or character |
| Privacy comprehension | Participant can accurately state that exact home addresses and precise locations are not shown before mutual acceptance |

## Success criteria

- At least 80% of participants complete Tasks 1 to 5 independently.
- At least 90% accurately explain the core privacy boundary.
- At least 90% accurately explain the limits of workplace verification.
- No critical safety misunderstanding remains unresolved after the session.
- Account deletion and reset are discoverable by at least 80% of participants.

### Severity scale

- **Critical**: creates a safety, privacy or irreversible-data-loss risk.
- **High**: prevents completion of a core task without help.
- **Medium**: causes repeated confusion or a significant delay.
- **Low**: cosmetic or minor usability issue.

## After sessions

1. Group observations into themes rather than relying on a single participant.
2. Prioritise critical and high issues before any wider pilot.
3. Record each accepted change, rejected change and reason in the feedback log.
4. Delete raw recordings and notes according to the approved research-retention schedule.
